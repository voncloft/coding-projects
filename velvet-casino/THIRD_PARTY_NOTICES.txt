Changes: decoded to mono, 44,100 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM WAV and discarded
source container metadata. No synthesized tones, pitch shifting, looping, or
repeated artificial collision pattern is added. Reproduce the conversion
with python3 tools/prepare_chip_sounds.py (requires FFmpeg).

The WAVs are compiled into the executable using a CMake-generated sounds.qrc. There
is no runtime download. These CC0 audio files and their included originals

OPTIONAL LOCAL PIXABAY AUDIO
The optional pack replaces the six default recordings with user-selected
Pixabay recordings and adds a setup intro. Version 1.0.8 uses OxidVideos
Shuffling Deck of Cards (522518) and annoyedCactus 8bit Music for Game (68698). Source links, authors, license terms and import/build
instructions are in licenses/Pixabay-Sounds.md. These recordings are under
the Pixabay Content License, not GPL or CC0. Standalone MP3/WAV recordings
are not included in the GitHub source. Local converted WAVs are git-ignored;
a custom build embeds them in the game. The public source builds without them.
