# Prototype acceptance and manufacturing release record

**Status at handoff: physical tests have not been performed.** Native electrical/CAD checks and software fixtures are documented separately. The specialist manufacturer owns the physical build, measurement and integration work below. Use `acceptance-record.csv` to record serial number, measured results, instrument/setup, operator, date and evidence path. Marking a row “pass” requires its evidence; “not tested” is not a pass.

## A — before making or powering hardware

1. Reconcile the purchased BOM against 829 positions, all 8 board exports, their hashes and the manual/SMT split. Check exact package pin numbering against datasheets for charger/regulators/gauge/PD/hub/level translators/CM4 connectors. Approve all `PROCUREMENT SPEC` MPN assignments and any stock alternatives; retain the original design value and rated voltage/dielectric/current requirements.
2. Approve actual layer stackup, copper thickness, return planes and 90-ohm USB /100-ohm DSI routing. Assess decoupling loops, switch-node coupling, current paths, QFN exposed-pad vias, copper and connector heating. Record any routing correction as a new revision and rerun DRC/ERC, harness and mechanical checks. Do not use a zero DRC count as an electrical-rating signoff.
3. Obtain pack charge/protection/temperature and transport documentation. Confirm the 2S 8.4 V/1 A charge profile and 6 A discharge limit are supported, clarify cell balancing, and verify thermistor mounting/curve. Verify fuse coordination and USB-C fault behaviour. Do not connect the real pack to an unqualified charger.
4. Build small fit samples covering switch/stabilizer/plate, USB openings, display mount and FFC corridor, rotary pocket, indicator light pipe, screw engagement and CM4 spacing. Verify connectors with actual mating housings and purchased part tolerances, not only the simplified 3D envelopes. Approve case material/process shrinkage and screw torque after coupon tests.

## B — bare boards, assembly and controlled bring-up

5. Bare-board open/short test against IPC-D-356. Verify fabrication dimensions, plated slots and mounting holes. Assembled-board AOI/X-ray as appropriate; prove no power-to-ground shorts or reversed electrolytic/LED/diode/IC orientations. Record resistance readings before power; capacitor charging means there is no universal resistance threshold for every rail.
6. Start with the real battery and external loads disconnected, using a current-limited battery simulator/bench arrangement under the electronics engineer's control. Verify 3.3 V always-on supply before connecting programming tools. Program RP2040 and ESP32 by the documented debug/ROM paths; never use an uncontrolled live USB supply to backfeed the rails. Confirm regulator defaults remain off until supervisor checks pass.
7. Measure3.3 V AON, 5.1 V CORE, 5.1 V IO, 3.3 V AUDIO and RGB rails at source and far load. Accept within each receiving component's datasheet limits under idle, startup, key/RGB/radio bursts and maximum granted loads; also record ripple/transient droop. Establish current limits from the schematic/device ratings before raising bench limits. Test AON radio burst headroom specifically: its revised 2 A class regulator is not an assumed infinite source.
8. Verify 5 V USB input fallback and 20 V PD offer detection independently from telemetry. Reject false/missing PD results without requesting excessive input current. With a suitable simulator, check charger voltage/current regulation, termination/recharge, open/hot/cold NTC, I2C timeout, watchdog reset, unplug/replug and pack protection behaviour. Only then repeat qualified tests with the selected protected pack and attached thermistor.
9. Test short/overload behaviour separately on every USB-A and watch output using a protected load fixture. Confirm no backfeed into the PC or PD input. Measure combinedUSB-A current limit and watch CC source advertisement. Attach the real Samsung USB-C charging dongle and confirm the watch charges without resets or overheating.

## C — user functions

10. Scan all 108 keys against the layout, including both Shift keys, numpad0/Plus/Enter, Print Screen, Bluetooth selectors and all 36 macro-bank assignments. Validate ghosting/diode orientation, rollover, host LEDs, held-key host switching and release behaviour. Program and read back a VIA change after a power cycle using the wired PC connection.
11. Illuminate each RGB LED individually, then use capped all-white and animation patterns. Verify ordering, colour channels, no flicker at radio/load bursts, regulator/connector temperatures and disabled-rail behaviour. Do not defeat the brightness cap for this test.
12. Pair three distinct hosts, switch between all slots repeatedly, reconnect after sleep/power loss, repeat passkey pairing, and verify reports reach only the selected authorized host. Test wireless range in the closed case with both final antennas installed. Confirm USB host mode via Fn+BT1 or the touch selector.
13. Exercise touchscreen corners, on-screen typing, macro editing/import/export, 12 pages/24 buttons bounds, shortcut/text/delay actions, colours/brightness, rotary macro-bank mode and screen-selection mode. Long press around 1 s changes mode; short press activates a selected on-screen control. Ensure a cancelled/host-switched queued macro does not execute later and held physical modifiers do not leak into macros.
14. Inject/report SOC values 100, 80, 79, 30, 29, 0 and invalid/stale telemetry. Require green at 80–100, mixed yellow at 30–79, red below 30 and off for invalid/stale data. Check visibility/mixing through the actual tiny light pipe at ordinary viewing angles. Calibrate gauge behaviour against actual pack discharge; boundary logic alone does not establish SOC accuracy.
15. With the PC USB data cable, confirm all three thumb drives/peripherals enumerate and sustain simultaneous read/write without errors. Verify repeated insertion/removal/suspend/resume, qualified cables and hubs. Confirm a failing/overcurrent device cannot reset the whole keyboard. USB2 high-speed testing, endpoint descriptors and signal quality require actual hardware evidence.
16. Play test left/right tones, keyboard clicks, PC USB stereo audio and local Plexamp, separately and mixed. Confirm channel order, sample rate 48 kHz stereo, acceptable latency/no underruns, no clipping at the intended maximum volume, and muted startup/shutdown. Check speaker clearance and rattles with the case closed. Confirm CPU/USB audio clock adaptation over a long run.
17. With the PC switched off, use the CM4 touchscreen and Plexamp against a still-running NAS/Plex server. Record network/library prerequisites and account claim. Test UI access to macros while music runs. The keyboard is not a Plex server and cannot access a powered-off library server.
18. Learn a known 38 kHz IR command from its existing remote, transmit toward the intended device, and save only after confirming success. Test timeout/malformed/imported-code limits. Configure a test Home Assistant integration for a Wi-Fi bulb and verify selected entity on/off/toggle. Preserve owner opt-in and never bundle credentials. Do not advertise universal RFID/RF learning.

## D — closed-case power, thermal and recovery

19. Run worst granted loads with charging, RGB, wireless, display, audio and USB peripherals. Record all input/pack currents, rail transients, heatsink/inductor/regulator/connector/pack/case temperatures until stable. Pass only within component and battery supplier limits, without throttle-induced malfunction, audible instability, brownouts or unsafe touch temperatures. Ambient, orientation and permitted airflow must be recorded. If cooling is insufficient, revise the enclosure/thermal design and recheck before closing this item.
20. Exercise all combinations of requested loads. Verify the 36 W PD reservation, battery budget, watch-first shedding when the other reservations exhaust headroom, fault messages and recovery. Record measured battery runtime under at least typing-only and display/music use; do not promise runtime from capacity arithmetic alone.
21. Exercise switch-off, low battery, interrupted communications, stale boot-generation acknowledgements, CM4 boot failure and forced emergency power cut. Require orderly filesystem shutdown where energy/time permits, no reboot loop at low charge, and explicit OFF/ON rearm after a latched low-battery stop. Inspect storage after repeated cycles.
22. Demonstrate RP2040 recovery, ESP32 ROM reflash and CM4 eMMC reimaging with the actual assembled access points. Verify all programmed-image hashes and factory-default configuration. Assign unique product identifiers/serials and use licensed USB VID/PID before commercial distribution; the included prototype IDs are not an allocation.
23. Finish a full closed-case fit/travel/rattle check, cable retention/pull test and appropriately specified transport/drop tests for the intended product. Decide applicable EMC/radio/electrical/battery compliance requirements for the actual sales market before any volume build.

## Return evidence and disposition

The prototype may be accepted only after every required row is passed or a specific owner-approved deviation is documented. Return measured results, photos, firmware/OS versions, purchased BOM, final cable lengths, torque limits, production notes and corrected native files. A finding requiring a component, copper, firmware or case change triggers focused retesting of the affected interfaces and a fresh final rule/fit/package audit.

## E — USB and headphone acceptance

24. USB inputs: qualify J6 alone, J1 alone, and both connected with J1 priority; no backfeed or host shorting. Measure complete input current at cold attach, reset, enumeration, suspend and hot replug on default USB, 1.5 A Type-C and 20 V / 3 A PD. Confirm HIZ/charge policy and hub 3.3 V stability; record supported source classes.

25. Programming: through J6 with J1 unplugged, enter RP2040 ROM mode after orderly CM4 halt and reflash the UF2. Demonstrate authenticated USB serial file transfer, guarded ESP32 update and physical recovery fallback. Verify hub continuity through reset and intact CM4 storage after repeated cycles.

26. Headphones: pair, disconnect, reconnect, forget and switch outputs on actual A2DP headphones. Play Plexamp with PC off, then mix USB PC audio and key sounds while ESP32 BLE HID remains connected. Verify closed-case range, channel order, latency, no underruns and UART reliability at the fixed clock.
